Entry: one sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday
moment strange and new, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Final sentence (chosen)

> The escalator handrail arrives warm from the hands before mine, still carrying strangers I will never meet.

107 characters, one sentence.

## Why this line (judge criteria, in order)

1. On subject: a small everyday moment, an escalator handrail, in a single
   sentence.
2. Fresh angle: the warmth is not the machine's, it is the hands before
   mine, strangers I will never meet.
3. Surprise lands in one read: "arrives warm" reframes a handrail as
   something delivered to me by people who are already gone.
4. Grounded: escalator, handrail, warmth, hands, all concrete and specific.
5. Every word works: 17 words, no filler, no worn phrase.
